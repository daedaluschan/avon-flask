## A letter to students

Dear Year 7 Student,

Welcome to the Latymer School! It is my pleasure to welcome you to our wonderful school and my hope is that you will enjoy the next seven years as part of our close-knit, diverse, and vibrant community.

We believe that we have a lot to offer you as new students, and we are proud of our long and successful history. As a member of the Latymer community, you will not only have the chance to develop your knowledge, but you will also be invited to enjoy a range of extra-curricular activities, from music and drama to competitive sport and a wide variety of student-led societies.

We have high expectations of everyone in the Latymer community. At Latymer we want you to be confident in your ability and considerate of others; these are our two key values. Being considerate and kind is very important as we want to make sure we create an inclusive and safe environment for all students regardless of their background. We also celebrate our similarities and our differences. We want everyone to feel valued, to be treated with respect and to know that we are all equally important at the school.

On that first exciting day when you join us in September, you will be greeted by your Head of Learning in the Great Hall and be directed to your form room with the rest of your class. Here, your form tutor will be waiting to meet you, and they will give you more information about your first day, including what lessons you will be doing, where you will go for those lessons, and when you will have lunch. On this day, you should arrive to school between 8.30am and 8.45am.

On the first day you will only need pens and pencils, a packed lunch if you have not already paid for a school lunch, and snacks to eat at break. We will give you a school planner and your timetable which will tell you all of the lessons you have each day. There will be lots to take in, but don't worry, you will be a Latymer pro before you know it!

Our school was founded in 1624 meaning we are over 400 years old. We are proud of our history and are excited that you are going to become a part of this, and perhaps more importantly part of our future. I feel privileged to work at Latymer and I hope you will feel privileged to be in our school too,

We look forward to seeing you in September and getting to know each of you personally.

I wish you a wonderful, relaxing summer holiday.

Mr Joseph Gilford, Headteacher

## The Latymer code of conduct

To help you settle in quickly, it is important for you to understand what is expected of all students in secondary school. At the start of the school year, you will also be asked to sign our 'Acceptable use of technology' agreement.

### Around school I will:

- Treat and respect each other as I would like to be treated and respected.
- Not insult or provoke others or enter into verbal or physical conflict.
- Take pride in how I look and adhere to the school uniform policy.
- Look after the school buildings and its equipment/resources.
- Eat and drink in areas set aside for this.
- Not use chewing or bubble gum.
- Look after the school environment both inside and outside by not creating litter.
- Be courteous towards others and aware of all users of the school when moving around.

### In class I will:

- Allow both the teacher to teach and other students to learn.
- Listen to the teacher whilst instructions are being given.
- Listen to others when it is their turn to speak.
- Not use terms of abuse, disrespect, or rude language.
- Not insult or deliberately provoke others.

### I am expected to:

- Come to class on time and notify my teacher in advance of any reason why I need to arrive late or leave early.
- Be suitably dressed according to the agreed school uniform policy.
- Enter classrooms in a respectful and orderly way, prepare for the lesson and wait in an appropriate manner for the teacher to arrive/lesson to start.
- Avoid the unnecessary distraction of other students or teachers.
- Be organised by having the correct equipment for each lesson and to have collected all books from my locker at registration, break and at the beginning and end of lunch time and not between lessons.
- Complete all homework/preparation work fully and on time.
- Be a good ambassador for the school whilst travelling to and from, and whilst in the surrounding area of the school premises.
- Treat staff and peers with respect at all times.

Everyone has the right to learn but no-one has the right to stop others from learning.

## Homework

Homework is an important part of school life, as it supports the work you are doing in class and helps you develop research skills. Completing homework encourages you to work independently - a very important skill for GCSEs and A Levels.

Once homework has been set, do it as soon as possible, so that you do not forget and tasks do not build up.

Each evening you may have two or three pieces of work from different subjects. Each piece of work should take 20-30 minutes, depending on the subject.

When the teacher sets homework, write it down in your student planner. The teacher will tell you when the work is due to be handed in, and it is really important that you meet this deadline. If, for some reason, you are unable to do so, you must let your teacher know.

At the end of the school day, check in your planner what homework you have, for which lessons, so that can take the books you need home. Get into the routine of doing your homework in a quiet place and at a specific time each day. For example, you could complete your homework before dinner, or between 6pm and 7pm.
When you have finished, pack your homework in your school bag to take the next day. Your teacher will expect you to have it in school for the appropriate lesson.

You need to get your student planner checked and signed by your parent/carer each weekend. Your form tutor will check and sign your planner once a week as well.

## Accessing school

You will be given a personalised card with a lanyard. You need to scan your card as you enter and exit the building in the morning and after school. You need to do this even if you are entering as part of a large group and the doors are already open. You should not leave the door open and you should not open the door to anyone you do not know.

The same card is used for the photocopier.
